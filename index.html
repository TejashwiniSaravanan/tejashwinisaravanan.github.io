About Me

I am a Healthcare Data Analyst based in Seattle, Washington, with over 3 years of professional experience and a Master of Science in Data Analytics from Seattle Pacific University, Class of 2025.

Healthcare is not just my target industry - it is the domain I am genuinely invested in. I have seen firsthand how the right data, presented clearly and at the right moment, can change the decisions clinicians make and the outcomes patients experience. That belief shapes how I approach every project - not as a technical exercise, but as an opportunity to build something that actually matters to the people on the receiving end of healthcare decisions.

Technically, I work across the full analytics stack. I build machine learning pipelines in Python and PySpark, design HIPAA-compliant cloud architectures on Google Cloud Platform, and deliver executive-facing dashboards in Tableau, Power BI, Looker Studio, and R Shiny. I understand both the engineering side - scalable pipelines, partitioned data lakes, real-time streaming - and the domain side - clinical trial design, patient risk stratification, EHR integration, and regulatory compliance.

I am actively seeking full-time roles in healthcare analytics, health informatics, and pharmaceutical data engineering, and open to relocation across the United States.

What I Do

Healthcare Analytics

Clinical trial patient selection, patient risk scoring, EHR data integration, and HIPAA-compliant cloud architecture. Experienced in working with sensitive medical datasets under regulatory constraints, designing pipelines that are both technically sound and clinically meaningful.

Data Analytics & Engineering

End-to-end data pipelines from raw ingestion to analytical output. Real-time streaming with Apache Spark Structured Streaming, watermarking, windowed aggregations, and partitioned Parquet data lakes. Scalable architectures built for production environments, not just classrooms.

Business Intelligence

Interactive dashboards in Tableau, Power BI, Looker Studio, R Shiny, and Flourish. Star Schema relational modeling, advanced DAX measures, and KPI design tailored for executive and clinical audiences. Visualization choices driven by the analytical question, not the tool.

Machine Learning

Supervised and unsupervised learning pipelines using scikit-learn, PySpark MLlib, Orange Data Mining, and RapidMiner. Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, K-Means Clustering, FP-Growth association rule mining, and Neural Networks - with honest, rigorous model evaluation and clinical interpretation of results.
